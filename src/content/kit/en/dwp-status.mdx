# dwp-status

Report Deep Work Plan status without executing.

<CmdStatus lang="en" class="not-prose my-8" />

## What it does

`dwp-status` is strictly read-only by contract. It summarizes progress, active blockers, checkpoints, and resource envelopes without making changes to any file.

## Usage

```
/dwp-status                      # list and summarize all plans
/dwp-status [plan]               # report details for a specific plan
```

## Behavior

1. **Four lifecycle dimensions:** reports format (`lite` or `full`), materialization status (`materializing`, `ready`, or `promoting`), review approval (`pending`, `approved`, or `pre_approved`), and execution progress (`pending`, `in_progress`, `blocked`, or `completed`).
2. **Repository-wide overview:** when called without arguments, lists all plans under `.dwp/plans/` with completion percentages, active task locators, and blocker summaries.
3. **Resource envelope:** displays consumed vs ceiling counters across tracked dimensions (`wall_clock`, retries, dispatches, spend, tokens) and indicates whether limits are actively enforced or advisory.
4. **Blocker diagnosis:** surfaces active blocker details from `state.json.blocked` categorized by the standard escalation taxonomy (classes C1–C7).
5. **Delegation observation:** summarizes open and completed subagent delegations and recorded peer exchanges.
