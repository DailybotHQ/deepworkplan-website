Deep Work Plan's recent releases make the harness easier to trust. The skill pins cross-repository installation examples, documents write boundaries, publishes checksums for released artifacts, and brings the local AI Diff Reviewer into the baseline.

The conformance floor is deliberately small: a repository can be fully AI-first without optional addons, while security and provenance remain explicit requirements. Addons can extend the harness, but they do not define its minimum standard.

Legacy repositories and plans are handled honestly: they keep their recorded shape and receive a finding or migration path rather than being silently treated as current.

Read the [skill changelog](https://github.com/DailybotHQ/deepworkplan-skill/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) and [trust statement](https://github.com/DailybotHQ/deepworkplan-skill/blob/main/skills/deepworkplan/TRUST.md).
